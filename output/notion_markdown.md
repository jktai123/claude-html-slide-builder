# Notion Skills for Claude
Notion Skills for Claude are step-by-step guides that teach Claude how to do real work in Notion. Instead of just answering questions, Skills help Claude complete entire workflows—structuring pages the way your team does, updating databases with the right info, following your brand guidelines, and working through your actual patterns.
Use them to turn a spec into an implementation plan, run research that ends up as a beautiful Notion doc, prep for meetings with context already pulled together, or capture decisions into your wiki or task database. It's a faster way to go from idea to done—and everything ends up organized, shareable, and ready for your team to use.

To get started: Download the .zip below, then upload each Skill to Claude. Once loaded, Claude will use them automatically when you're working in Notion.

## Install the skills in Claude

### Before you begin
  1. You need access to Claude and the ability to upload Skills in Claude.
  1. Download the Notion Skills .zip files below.

### In Claude
  1. Open Settings > Capabilities
  1. Go to Skills
  1. Upload each Notion Skill .zip file from the options below.
  1. Click the …  option to Try in chat to get started

For more information, please read ‣’s launch blog and check out Anthropic’s Help Center articles What are Skills? and Using Skills in Claude.
